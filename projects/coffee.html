Coffee Pricing & Cost of Living

A U.S. pricing analysis connecting coffee menu prices with state-level cost-of-living data to explore how local economic conditions relate to what consumers pay.

PythonSQLTableauPower BI
150+price observations
50states analyzed
3beverage types

What I did

  • Cleaned and structured state-level coffee pricing data in Python.
  • Used SQL joins to combine pricing data with cost-of-living indices.
  • Built interactive Tableau and Power BI visualizations for regional comparison.

What I found

The analysis showed a positive association between higher cost of living and higher coffee prices, with clear regional differences visible across the U.S.
