Inequality in Smoking Prevalence

An interactive public-health analysis examining how smoking prevalence varies by geography, education, age, race, and sex.

What I did

  • Cleaned, merged, and reshaped multiple public datasets into a unified analysis table.
  • Used SQL to aggregate smoking and quit-attempt metrics across demographic groups.
  • Built interactive visualizations to make inequality patterns easier to explore.

Focus

The project was designed to make public-health disparities visible and understandable rather than presenting smoking prevalence as a single national average.
