03 · HOW I THINK

How I think,
in four lines.

Four principles I keep coming back to, whether I'm analyzing a dataset or building with AI.

01

Question the metric.

A result is more valuable when you understand what produced it.

02

Clarity is the deliverable.

The best analysis is something someone else can understand and act on.

03

Get something working. Then improve it.

I learn by making things, finding what doesn't work, and trying again.

04

The user is the point.

A simple solution that solves the problem is better than a flashy one that doesn't.
